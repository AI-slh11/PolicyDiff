Guarantees

Five Non-Negotiable E&O Guarantees

Engineered specifically to protect commercial brokers from coverage comparison oversights.

01

Every Value Has A Citation

Every extracted limit, deductible, and flagged diff carries exact coordinate citations: document, page number, and bounding box. If a figure cannot be cited, it is never shown.

02

Rule-Based Policy Matching

Numeric limits, sub-limits, and ISO form codes are compared via strict code rules, not generative guesswork. The comparison logic is 100% deterministic.

03

Recall Over Precision

Missing an exclusionary endorsement is catastrophic for agency E&O liability. The ingestion pipeline optimizes strictly for recall across all policy schedules.

04

Mandatory Human Sign-Off

PolicyDiff assists human review; it never replaces professional judgment. Every critical discrepancy must be accepted or flagged with notes before audit export is unlocked.

05

Strict Retention & Zero Model Training

Uploaded commercial policies are sensitive records. Files are encrypted at rest, permanently purged after a configurable agency retention window (default 90 days), and strictly never used for AI training.

Enterprise Security & Agency Compliance

Engineered with bank-grade encryption, complete agency tenant isolation, and strict document lifecycle policies.

Document Ingestion & Citation Grounding

  • • Dual-Mode Ingestion: Extracts native PDF text layers with coordinates; falls back to high-resolution OCR for scanned binders and quotes.
  • • File Integrity Checks: Pre-upload validation verifies document formats and payload integrity before storage.
  • • Two-Pass Grounding Verification: Automatically re-reads cited bounding coordinates to confirm numerical limit accuracy before diffing.
  • • Rule-Based Summary Validation: Strictly rejects any generated summary text that references figures absent from the underlying source page.
